Reflections on Collaboration & Iterative Design

Mutual Dependence of Dataset Modeling and Algorithm Design:

As emphasized in the course guidelines, algorithm design is inherently an iterative process. During our initial implementation of Step 3, we attempted simple substring matching (e.g., checking if "music" was present in the genre string). However, testing quickly revealed that this caused films classified solely as Musical (such as Anand) to be erroneously counted under Music.

This realization prompted us to revisit Step 2 and Step 3: rather than altering the dataset to force artificial single-genre constraints, we refined our data model to treat genres as comma-separated tokens and modified the algorithm to split and trim each tag individually. This preserved the multi-genre richness of cinema while ensuring 100% precision during evaluation.
